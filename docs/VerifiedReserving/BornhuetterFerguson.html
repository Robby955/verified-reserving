    Bornhuetter-Ferguson on the chain-ladder pattern #

    The Bornhuetter-Ferguson (BF) method replaces the chain-ladder ultimate by an a priori ultimate U (typically premium times an expected loss ratio) and applies the chain-ladder development pattern to it:

    R^BF_i = U (1 - 1 / ∏_{k=d}^{n-2} f̂_k), Ĉ^BF_{i,n-1} = C_{i,d} + R^BF_i.

    Deterministic facts proved here:

    • the BF reserve is linear in the a priori ultimate;
    • with U equal to the chain-ladder ultimate, BF returns the chain-ladder ultimate exactly (the well-known "BF is CL with a different prior");
    • the percentage-reported factor 1 / ∏ f̂_k lies in [0, 1] when every factor is at least 1, so the BF reserve is a fraction of U.
