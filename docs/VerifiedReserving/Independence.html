    From independence across accident years to the D_k form of the assumptions #

    Mack (1993) states his assumptions on a single accident year: for accident year i, conditionally on that year's own history C_{i,0}, …, C_{i,k},

    (M1row) E[C_{i,k+1} | σ(C_{i,0}, …, C_{i,k})] = f_k C_{i,k}, (M3row) E[(C_{i,k+1} - f_k C_{i,k})² | σ(C_{i,0}, …, C_{i,k})] = σ_k² C_{i,k},

    and separately assumes (M2), that the accident years are independent. Every theorem in the library instead uses the D_k-conditioned forms Mack1 and Mack3, where D_k is the σ-algebra of everything observed up to development year k, all accident years together. Mack's papers pass from the first form to the second in one sentence. This file proves the passage.

    The mathematical content is a general fact about conditional expectation: enlarging the conditioning σ-algebra by an independent one changes nothing. If m₁ ≤ m₁', f is m₁'-measurable and m₂ is independent of m₁', then

    E[f | m₁ ⊔ m₂] = E[f | m₁].

    Mathlib has the special case m₁ = ⊥ (condExp_indep_eq: conditioning on an independent σ-algebra returns the mean). The join version is proved here by the characterisation of conditional expectation, testing on the π-system of rectangles a ∩ b with a ∈ m₁ and b ∈ m₂; on such a rectangle both integrals factor, by condExp_indep_eq applied to the indicator 1_a f.

    Applied with m₁ = row i up to development year k, m₁' = all of row i, and m₂ = the other rows up to k, this turns (M1row) into (M1) and (M3row) into (M3), given the hypothesis RowsGenerateD that D_k is generated by the rows' histories up to k (which is what D_k means) and the hypothesis RowsIndep that the rows are independent (Mack's (M2)).

    Conditioning on an independent enlargement changes nothing. If f is measurable for m₁', the σ-algebra m₂ is independent of m₁', and m₁ ≤ m₁', then E[f | m₁ ⊔ m₂] = E[f | m₁] almost surely.

    This is the lemma behind Mack's passage from a row-conditioned assumption to the same statement conditioned on the whole observed triangle.

                    Mack's (M2') from independence across accident years. The conditional uncorrelatedness of residuals from different accident years, which Variance.lean and SigmaUnbiased.lean take as an assumption, is not an extra assumption: it follows from (M2) and the row-conditioned (M1row).

                    The argument conditions on the observed data together with the whole of the other accident year: the other year's residual comes out of the conditional expectation, what is left is the conditional mean of row i's residual given a σ-algebra that adds nothing about row i, which is zero by (M1row); the tower property brings the statement back down to D_k.
