    Stochastic Bühlmann-Straub credibility #

    Bühlmann and Straub, "Glaubwürdigkeit für Schadensätze" (1970), assume in Sections 3 and 5 that a contract has a latent risk parameter, that its loss ratios have a common conditional mean, and that their conditional variance is sigma^2(Theta) / P. Section 6, pp. 118-120, minimizes expected squared prediction error over unbiased linear predictors. Display (4), p. 123, is the familiar credibility blend.

    This file gives the one-risk moment calculation behind that blend. The model records the conditional first and second moments actually consumed by the proof. Conditional cross moments replace the source's stronger conditional independence assumption. From those hypotheses the weighted experience has conditional process variance sigma^2(Theta) / P. Averaging the latent variance gives the structural process parameter v, so prediction loss for the latent mean is

    (1 - z)^2 * w + z^2 * v / P.

    Multiplication by P gives the deterministic coefficient loss P * w * (1-z)^2 + v * z^2. Completing its square produces z = P*w/(v+P*w), and the resulting predictor is exactly buhlmannStraubEstimate. The collective mean and both variance components are model parameters. Their empirical estimation in Section 7 is not used here.
