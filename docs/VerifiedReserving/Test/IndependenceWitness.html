    A finite witness for independence across accident years #

    The nontrivial model already has one Rademacher shock per accident year on eight equiprobable outcomes. This file verifies the stronger row-level hypotheses from Independence.lean: the row sigma-algebras are mutually independent and their observed histories generate the filtration. These are the finite-model forms of Mack (1993), equations (1) and (2), and the B_k filtration used in the proof of Theorem 2 on pp. 214-216.
