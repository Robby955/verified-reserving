    A strict counterexample: the two estimation-error estimators differ #

    Catalogue.lean proves mackEstimation ≤ bbmwEstimation and gives the exact difference Ĉ² (∏(1 + a_k) - 1 - ∑ a_k). This file exhibits a concrete run-off triangle on which the difference is strictly positive, so the two published estimators of the estimation error are not the same estimator.

    The triangle has four accident years and four development years. Its individual development factors in the first two columns are not all equal, so σ̂_0² > 0 and σ̂_1² > 0, hence a_0 > 0 and a_1 > 0 along the row of the youngest accident year, and the remainder ∏(1+a_k) - 1 - ∑ a_k contains the product a_0 a_1 > 0.

    Every number below is checked by norm_num from the definitions; nothing is asserted numerically without proof.
