    Mack (1999): the recursive form of the standard error equals the 1993 closed form #

    Mack, ASTIN Bulletin 29 (1999) 361-366, rewrites the 1993 estimator as a recursion along the row of accident year i, starting from 0 on the latest diagonal:

    s.e.(Ĉ_{i,k+1})² = Ĉ_{i,k}² (s.e.(F_{i,k})² + s.e.(f̂_k)²) + s.e.(Ĉ_{i,k})² f̂_k²

    with, for α = 1 and unit weights, s.e.(F_{i,k})² = σ̂_k² / Ĉ_{i,k} and s.e.(f̂_k)² = σ̂_k² / S_k. The paper states that the recursion "leads to" the 1993 closed form. This file proves that statement: after i steps the recursion returns exactly msep as defined in ChainLadder.lean, provided the development factors along the row are nonzero. This is catalogue row 1 of the variant catalogue: Mack 1999 (α = 1) is literally equal to Mack 1993.
