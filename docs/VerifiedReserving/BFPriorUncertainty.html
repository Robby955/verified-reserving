    Uncertainty in the Bornhuetter-Ferguson prior #

    Mack (2008), "The Prediction Error of Bornhuetter/Ferguson," describes the a priori ultimate estimate and the selected development pattern as practically independent and uses the variance formula for a product of independent random variables (Section 4, properties (b)-(e), pp. 94-95; Section 5, pp. 95-96).

    Here the estimated development pattern is the chain-ladder pattern already used by this repository's bfReserve specialization and is measurable for the observed run-off triangle. The exact independence hypothesis below strengthens Mack's practical-independence description. Under it, the reserve has exact conditional mean and variance formulas given the triangle. This is the prior-uncertainty component of Mack's product-variance calculation; it does not assert the rest of his prediction-error approximation.
