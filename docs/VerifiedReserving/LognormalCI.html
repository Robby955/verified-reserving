    Mack's moment-matched lognormal approximation #

    This file formalizes the parameter conversion in Thomas Mack, "Measuring the Variability of Chain Ladder Reserve Estimates," CAS Forum, Spring 1994, Chapter 4, equation (10), pp. 118-119. The paper uses the conversion to form a lognormal approximation to the reserve distribution. The two results below verify the mean and variance algebra; they do not assert that a reserve is lognormally distributed.
