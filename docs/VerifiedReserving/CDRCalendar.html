    Calendar-year conditioning for the claims development result #

    Merz and Wüthrich (2008) condition on the triangle available after a calendar diagonal: at time t, the observed cells in the modeled n by n square are exactly those with i + j ≤ t. The earlier CDR modules used the development-year filtration D k. This file defines the source's calendar-year filtration and proves the corresponding exact results from Mack's row-conditioned assumptions and accident-year independence.

    For row i and k < n, calendar time i + k contains exactly that row's history through development year k, joined with observations from the other rows. Independence therefore makes conditioning on the calendar triangle equivalent to conditioning on row i's own history. This gives the calendar-year residual identity and its conditional second moment without adding a stochastic assumption.
