# Sources for the Schedule P audit

All retrieved 2026-08-25 with `download.sh` (curl). Hashes are SHA-256 of the files as downloaded; `data/SHA256SUMS` is regenerated by the script.

## The CAS loss reserving database (Meyers and Shi)

Landing page as cited in the literature (returns "Page not found" as of 2026-08-25):
https://www.casact.org/research-resources/research/loss-reserving-data

Current landing page (CAS Reserve Research Working Group; the 1988-1997 files are unchanged, and a second set with accident years 1998-2007 was added in March 2026 under `/sites/default/files/2026-03/`, not used here):
https://www.casact.org/publications-research/research/research-resources/loss-reserving-data-pulled-naic-schedule-p

The page states that the original data sets were "updated on September 1, 2011" and "compiled by Glenn G. Meyers, PhD, FCAS; and Peng Shi, PhD, ASA", and asks that publications acknowledge that the files were accessed from the CAS web page. Inclusion criteria stated on the page: the insurer is present in Schedule P of year 1997 and of the subsequent years; the 10 accident years by 10 development lags are complete; the 1997 Schedule P claims match those from subsequent years; net premiums written are not zero for all years.

File layout: one row per (GRCODE, AccidentYear, DevelopmentLag), 100 rows per company (10 accident years 1988-1997 by 10 lags), columns GRCODE, GRNAME, AccidentYear, DevelopmentYear, DevelopmentLag, IncurLoss_X, CumPaidLoss_X, BulkLoss_X, EarnedPremDIR_X, EarnedPremCeded_X, EarnedPremNet_X, Single, PostedReserve97_X, with X = B (ppauto), D (wkcomp), C (comauto), F2 (medmal), R1 (prodliab), h1 (othliab). Values in thousands of dollars, net of reinsurance.

What the audit uses: `CumPaidLoss_X`, upper triangle only, i.e. rows with AccidentYear + DevelopmentLag - 1 <= 1997 (55 cells per company), the data available at year-end 1997. The lower triangle (45 cells per company, the realized outcomes from later Schedule P filings) is present in the same files and is left untouched for a later backtest.

Companies per file: ppauto 146, wkcomp 132, comauto 158, medmal 34, prodliab 70, othliab 239 (779 in total). Every company has exactly 100 rows and a complete upper triangle.

## Reference implementations reused

- `../reproduce_mack1993.py`: `mack()` (the formulas of VerifiedReserving/ChainLadder.lean and Msep.lean) and `load_triangle()`; reproduced against Mack (1993) Tables 2 and 3 in the self-test.
- `../case_study/raa_case_study.py`: `last_sigma_loglinear()` (R ChainLadder `estimate.sigma` rule), `msep_with_sigma()`, `estimation_error_terms()`; reproduced against the published `MackChainLadder(RAA, est.sigma="Mack")` totals in the self-test.
- Published values: `../data/mack1993_published_results.json`, `../case_study/published_r_chainladder.json`.

## Other public copies (not used)
