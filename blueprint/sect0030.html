30 Reference computation

Mack’s 1993 example [ 1 ] , based on the Taylor and Ashe triangle [ 19 ] , is reproduced to every printed digit (nine reserves, the total 18,681 thousand, all standard-error percentages) by a dependency-free script implementing the definitions above in floating point. The Lean definitions are over \(\mathbb {R}\) and noncomputable; the script is the reference computation, not the Lean code.
