18 Bühlmann-Straub credibility

Bühlmann and Straub, Glaubwürdigkeit für Schadensätze (1970), minimize expected squared prediction error over unbiased linear estimators in Section 6, pages 118–120, and derive the normal equations in display (1). Display (4), page 123, gives the exposure-weighted individual mean, the credibility factor, and the credibility-weighted blend. The deterministic loss below is an algebraic representation of the one-risk normal equation, not the expected prediction-error objective of the paper.

For a finite sample with exposure weights \(P_l\) and observations \(X_l\), set

\[ P_\bullet =\sum _l P_l,\qquad T=\sum _l P_lX_l,\qquad \bar X=T/P_\bullet , \qquad Z=\frac{P_\bullet w}{v+P_\bullet w}. \]

For collective mean \(m\), process variance \(v\), and between-risk variance \(w\), define

\[ \widehat m=\frac{wT+vm}{v+P_\bullet w},\qquad L(t)=w\sum _lP_l(X_l-t)^2+v(t-m)^2. \]

The quantities \(m\), \(v\), and \(w\) are inputs.

If \(v+P_\bullet w\ne 0\), then

\[ (v+P_\bullet w)\widehat m=wT+vm, \qquad L(t)=L(\widehat m)+(v+P_\bullet w)(t-\widehat m)^2. \]

Consequently, \(L(\widehat m)\le L(t)\) for every \(t\) whenever \(v+P_\bullet w{\gt}0\).

Proof ▶

Expand the weighted squared deviations, substitute the normal equation, and use nonnegativity of a square.

If \(P_\bullet \ne 0\) and \(v+P_\bullet w\ne 0\), then

\[ \widehat m=Z\bar X+(1-Z)m. \]

If \(P_\bullet \), \(v\), and \(w\) are nonnegative and the denominator is positive, then \(0\le Z\le 1\).

Proof ▶

Clear the two nonzero denominators and normalize the resulting polynomial identity. The interval follows by comparing the nonnegative numerator with its denominator.
