22 Mack 1999: row assumptions and active weights

Mack’s weighted CL2 is conditioned on the history of one accident year. The full-filtration formula and the cross-accident-year cancellation therefore come from the same independence step as their Mack 1993 counterparts. Zero weights are handled by fixing the active observations before fitting the weighted mean.

Definition 22.1 Row-conditioned weighted CL2
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.Mack3WRow

The weights are measurable for the accident year’s history and

\[ \mathbb E\! \left[(F_{i,k}-f_k)^2\mid C_{i,0},\ldots ,C_{i,k}\right] = \frac{\sigma _k^2}{w_{i,k}C_{i,k}^{\alpha }}. \]
Theorem 22.2 Transport of weighted CL2
✓
#

Uses

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.mack3W_of_mack3WRow

If the rows are independent and generate the observed-data filtration, then row-conditioned weighted CL2 implies Mack3W, its form conditioned on the full data through development year \(k\).

Proof ▶

Split the filtration into row \(i\) and the other rows. Conditioning on the independent enlargement leaves the squared factor residual unchanged.

Theorem 22.3 Cross-factor terms from independent rows
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.mack2Factor'_of_rows

Under row-conditioned CL1, nonzero cumulative claims and integrability, row independence gives

\[ \mathbb E[(F_{i,k}-f_k)(F_{j,k}-f_k)\mid D_k]=0, \qquad i\ne j. \]
Proof ▶

Enlarge \(D_k\) by the whole of row \(j\), take its residual outside the conditional expectation, use the zero conditional mean for row \(i\), and apply the tower property.

Definition 22.4 Active contributors and variance estimator
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.activeContributors
  • VerifiedReserving.sigma2WOn
  • VerifiedReserving.sigma2WActive
  • VerifiedReserving.Mack3WOn

The active set is the fixed subset of contributors with nonzero deterministic weight. For an active set \(A\),

\[ \widehat\sigma _{k,A}^2 =\frac{1}{|A|-1}\sum _{i\in A}w_{i,k}C_{i,k}^{\alpha } (F_{i,k}-\widehat f_{k,A})^2. \]

When cumulative claims are nonzero, membership in \(A\) is equivalent to a nonzero weighted volume. Weighted CL2 on \(A\) imposes no condition on cells excluded from \(A\).

Theorem 22.5 Active weighted variance is unbiased
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 22.1
  • Definition 22.4

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.condExp_sigma2WActiveRv

For at least two active observations, weighted CL2, the cross-factor condition, nonzero active cumulative claims and weighted volumes, and the stated integrability hypotheses imply

\[ \mathbb E[\widehat\sigma _{k,A}^2\mid D_k]=\sigma _k^2. \]

The set \(A\) is fixed; the theorem makes no claim for a selection rule that depends on the outcome.
