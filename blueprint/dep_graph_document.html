Theorem 5.5Conditional unbiasedness of the development factor

Assume (M1), that \(S_k \neq 0\) almost surely, that each \(C_{i,k+1}\) and \(\hat f_k\) are integrable. Then \(E[\hat f_k \mid \mathcal{D}_k] = f_k\) almost surely.

Theorem 5.7Uncorrelated development factors, conditional form

Under (M1), on a finite measure space, for \(j {\lt} k\) (with the integrability and \(S \neq 0\) hypotheses of Theorem 5.5 for both columns and \(\hat f_j \hat f_k\) integrable), \(E[\hat f_j \hat f_k \mid \mathcal{D}_j] = f_j f_k\) almost surely.

Theorem 5.15Estimation variance of the development factor

Under (M3) and (M2’), with the residual products integrable, on \(\{ S_k \neq 0\} \), \(E[(\hat f_k - f_k)^2 \mid \mathcal{D}_k] = \sigma _k^2 / S_k\) (Mack 1993, proof of Theorem 3; Mack 1999, \(\mathrm{s.e.}(\hat f_k)^2 = \hat\sigma _k^2/S_k\)).
