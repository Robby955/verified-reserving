17 Prior uncertainty in Bornhuetter-Ferguson

Mack (2008), The Prediction Error of Bornhuetter/Ferguson, calls the selected pattern and the a priori ultimate practically independent in Section 4, property (b), pages 94–95, then uses the product-variance formula on pages 95–96. This chapter freezes the observed chain-ladder pattern of Definition 4.1 and isolates the exact prior contribution. Independence below strengthens the paper’s description.

Definition 17.1 Random BF reserve
✓

  • VerifiedReserving.RandomTriangle.bfOutstandingFractionRv
  • VerifiedReserving.RandomTriangle.bfReservePriorRv

For observed triangle \(X\), accident year \(i\), and random a priori ultimate \(U\),

\[ A_i = 1 - \frac{1}{\operatorname {cdf}_i(X)}, \qquad R^{BF,U}_i = A_i U. \]

The coefficient \(A_i\) is measurable for the observed-data sigma-algebra.

Definition 17.2 Independent prior
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 17.1

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.BFPriorIndependent

The sigma-algebra generated by \(U\) is independent of the observed-data sigma-algebra. The variance below is mathlib’s standard \(\operatorname {Var}(U)\), equal on a probability space to \(\int (U-\int U\, d\mu )^2d\mu \) under the stated measurability hypothesis.

Theorem 17.3 Conditional mean of the random-prior BF reserve
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 17.1
  • Definition 17.2

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.condExp_bfReservePriorRv

On a probability space, if \(U\) is strongly measurable and integrable, \(A_iU\) is integrable, and the prior is independent of the observed triangle, then

\[ E[R^{BF,U}_i \mid \mathrm{obsSigma}] = A_i E[U] \]

almost surely. This is the conditional form of the expectation calculation on Mack’s page 95.

Proof ▶

Pull the observed coefficient out of the conditional expectation, then condition the independent prior on the observed-data sigma-algebra.

Theorem 17.4 Conditional variance contributed by the prior
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 17.1
  • Definition 17.2
  • Theorem 17.3

Lean declarations

  • VerifiedReserving.condVar_bfReservePriorRv

If the prior and the resulting BF reserve are square-integrable, then

\[ E\left[(R^{BF,U}_i - A_iE[U])^2 \mid \mathrm{obsSigma}\right] = A_i^2\operatorname {Var}(U) \]

almost surely. This is the prior-uncertainty component of the product-variance formula on Mack’s pages 95–96; it does not state the other components of the paper’s prediction-error approximation.

Proof ▶

Rewrite the centred reserve as \(A_i(U-E[U])\), pull \(A_i^2\) out of the conditional expectation, and use independence for the squared centred prior.
