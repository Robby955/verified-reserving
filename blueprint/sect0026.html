26 Stochastic Bühlmann-Straub credibility

Bühlmann and Straub [ 8 ] , Sections 3, 5, and 6, model a loss ratio \(X_l\) using a latent risk parameter \(\Theta \). This chapter fixes the contract conditions. Conditional on \(\Theta \), the loss ratios have common mean \(M=\mu (\Theta )\) and process variance \(\sigma ^2(\Theta )/P_l\), where \(P_l\) is exposure. The structural process parameter is \(v=E[\sigma ^2(\Theta )]\). Their Section 6 minimizes expected squared prediction error. This chapter proves the one-risk moment calculation and obtains the credibility factor in display (4), page 123. The scanned primary source is available from E-Periodica under DOI 10.5169/seals-967024.

Let \(\mathcal G=\sigma (\Theta )\), \(S=\sigma ^2(\Theta )\), and \(e_l=X_l-M\). For the finite exposure sample, the model records

\[ E[X_l\mid \mathcal G]=M,\qquad E[e_l^2\mid \mathcal G]=\frac S{P_l},\qquad E[e_le_r\mid \mathcal G]=0\quad (l\ne r), \]

together with \(E[S]=v\), \(E[M]=m\), \(E[(M-m)^2]=w\), positive exposures, measurability, and the needed integrability. The source assumes conditional independence; the Lean proof records only the conditional cross moments it consumes.

The weighted residual total has conditional mean zero and conditional second moment \(SP\). When the total exposure \(P\) is nonzero, it follows that

\[ E[(\bar X-M)^2]=\frac vP, \qquad E[(M-m)(\bar X-M)]=0. \]

Every predictor \(m+z(\bar X-m)\) in the scalar family has expectation \(m\), so it satisfies the source’s expectation-unbiasedness constraint.

Proof ▶

Scale each residual by its exposure. Conditional cross moments remove the off-diagonal terms, and \(P_l^2(S/P_l)=SP_l\). Pulling the centered latent mean through conditional expectation removes the remaining cross term.

For every scalar coefficient \(z\) and nonzero total exposure,

\[ R(z)=(1-z)^2w+z^2\frac vP, \qquad PR(z)=Q(z). \]

Thus the deterministic coefficient quadratic is derived from the expected squared prediction loss, rather than postulated.

Proof ▶

Expand \(M-\widehat M_z=(1-z)(M-m)-z(\bar X-M)\) and apply Theorem 26.3.

If \(v+Pw{\gt}0\), then

\[ Q(z)=Q(Z)+(v+Pw)(z-Z)^2, \qquad Z=\frac{Pw}{v+Pw}. \]

For \(P{\gt}0\), \(Z\) minimizes the actual expected squared prediction loss. The predictor \(m+Z(\bar X-m)\) is pointwise equal to buhlmannStraubEstimate. Supplying the source’s empirical portfolio mean as \(m\) gives its display (4) estimator.

Proof ▶

Complete the square in \(Q\), use positivity to compare scaled expected losses, and apply the deterministic credibility-form identity.

There is a two-outcome probability model with one unit exposure, constant latent mean \(1\), and observed loss ratio \(0\) or \(2\) with equal probability. It satisfies the moment model with process variance \(v=1{\gt}0\) and has a nonconstant observation. Its between-risk variance is zero; the general theorems do not require that specialization.

Proof ▶

Conditional expectation given the trivial \(\sigma \)-algebra is the two-point average.
