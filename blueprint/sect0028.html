28 Panjer recursion

Panjer, Recursive Evaluation of a Family of Compound Distributions [ 10 ] , equation (1), assumes claim-count probabilities satisfying \(p_n=p_{n-1}(a+b/n)\). For a severity distribution supported on the positive integers, equation (5) gives a direct recursion for the aggregate mass.

Definition 28.1 Formal generating series and compound mass

For a coefficient sequence \(p\), let \(P(X)=\sum _n p_nX^n\). For a severity sequence \(f\) with \(f_0=0\), the compound series is \(G(X)=P(F(X))\). The predicate PanjerFrequency is the division-free recurrence

\[ (n+1)p_{n+1}=(a(n+1)+b)p_n. \]

The definition panjerEquation5 is the finite sum printed in the source.

The count recurrence is equivalent to

\[ P'=(a+b)P+aXP'. \]

Formal substitution and the power-series chain rule give, for \(i\geq 1\),

\[ g_i=\sum _{j=1}^{i}\left(a+b\frac ji\right)f_jg_{i-j}, \qquad g_0=p_0. \]

The coefficient of \(P(F(X))\) is also the convolution mixture in the source’s equation (6).

Proof ▶

Convert the count recurrence to a formal differential equation, substitute the severity series, apply the formal chain rule, and compare coefficients.

The geometric coefficients \((1-q)q^n\) satisfy the Panjer recurrence with \((a,b)=(q,0)\). For deterministic severity one, composition leaves the count mass unchanged, and the resulting geometric instance satisfies equation (5) at every index.

Proof ▶

Expand the geometric recurrence. The unit-severity series is \(X\), so substitution by it is the identity.
