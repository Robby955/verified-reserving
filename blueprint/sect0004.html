If \(\mathrm{cdf}_i \neq 0\) and \(U = \hat C_{i,n-1}\), then \(\hat C^{BF}_{i,n-1} = \hat C_{i,n-1}\) and \(R^{BF}_i = \hat R_i\).

Proof ▶

\(\hat C_{i,n-1} = C_{i,d}\, \mathrm{cdf}_i\), so \(U(1 - 1/\mathrm{cdf}_i) = C_{i,d}\mathrm{cdf}_i - C_{i,d}\).

If every development factor along the row is at least \(1\), then \(\mathrm{cdf}_i \ge 1\) and, for \(U \ge 0\), \(0 \le R^{BF}_i(U) \le U\).
